Explore Student Services

Discover facilities and support that may be available to students.

Library Services

Students may use the library to access textbooks, reference books and study materials.

Library rules, borrowing limits and opening hours depend on the college.

Contact the librarian for membership and book availability.

Computer Lab and Internet

Computer labs may provide access to computers, software and internet facilities.

Use college equipment responsibly and follow lab timings.

Ask the lab coordinator about available facilities and access rules.

Student Helpdesk

The student helpdesk can guide students toward the appropriate college department.

You may ask about academic procedures, forms, schedules and general student queries.

For official decisions, contact the relevant department directly.

Certificates and Documents

Students may need to request bonafide certificates, transcripts or other academic documents.

Application forms, processing times and fees depend on college policy.

Contact the administration office for the correct procedure.

Career Guidance and Placement

Career services may provide resume guidance, interview preparation and career counselling.

Placement activities and internship opportunities vary by college and course.

Contact the placement cell for current opportunities and eligibility requirements.

Sports and Student Activities

Student activities may include sports, cultural events, clubs and competitions.

Check college announcements for schedules, registration and participation rules.
