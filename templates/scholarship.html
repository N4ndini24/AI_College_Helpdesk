Scholarship Information

Explore scholarship options and financial assistance for students.

Types of Scholarships

Merit-based: For students who meet academic performance requirements.

Income-based: For eligible students whose family income meets specified criteria.

Category-based: For eligible students under applicable government schemes.

Institutional: Scholarships or fee assistance offered by some colleges.

Availability and conditions vary by scheme.

Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility may depend on academic marks, family income, course, category and residence.

Some schemes require students to be enrolled in a recognized institution.

Read the official scheme guidelines to confirm your eligibility.

Required Documents

Depending on the scheme, you may need:

Previous examination mark sheets

Income certificate

Applicable category certificate

Bank account details

Admission or bonafide certificate

Identity proof and photograph

The exact list depends on the scholarship.

How to Apply

Find a scholarship for which you may be eligible.

Read the official eligibility rules and deadline.

Register on the authorized application portal, if required.

Complete the form and upload the required documents.

Submit the application and save the acknowledgement number.

Application Status and Renewal

Use the official scholarship portal to check application status, if this feature is available.

Some scholarships require renewal each academic year.

Meet the renewal deadline and academic requirements specified by the scheme.

Where to Find Official Scholarships

Students in Maharashtra can check the official MahaDBT portal for applicable schemes:

MahaDBT — Maharashtra Government Portal

Students should verify the scheme details, eligibility and deadline on the official portal.
